One-time tasks to get your ExamTools account ready before your first session.

- **[Create your account](create-account)** — Register on HamStudy, verify your callsign, and access ExamTools.
- **[Add your VEC accreditation](add-vec-accreditation)** — Link your VE credentials so you can participate in sessions.
- **[Add your signature](add-signature)** — Set up the digital signature used on NCVEC Form 605 and certificates.
