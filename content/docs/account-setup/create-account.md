ExamTools uses HamStudy for account management. If you already have a HamStudy account, you can use it to log in to ExamTools directly.

## Prerequisites

- Your FCC callsign
- An email address

## Steps

1. Go to [hamstudy.org](https://hamstudy.org) and click **Sign Up**.

   <SCREENSHOT: HamStudy homepage with Sign Up button highlighted>

2. Enter your callsign, email address, and a password, then click **Create Account**.

   <SCREENSHOT: HamStudy registration form>

3. Check your email for a verification message and click the confirmation link.

4. Go to [exam.tools](https://exam.tools) and click **Sign In**. Use the same credentials you just created.

   <SCREENSHOT: ExamTools sign-in page>

5. The first time you sign in, ExamTools will ask you to verify your callsign against FCC records. Confirm that the name and callsign displayed match yours, then click **Confirm**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Callsign verification screen>

## Verify

After signing in, you should land on the ExamTools dashboard. Your callsign appears in the top-right corner of the page.
