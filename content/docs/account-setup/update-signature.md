A saved signature is an optional convenience for future digital signing. You can replace it at any time, or choose not to save one and draw a signature when you sign documents.

## Prerequisites

- A verified [ExamTools account](create-account)

## Update your saved signature

1. Go to [exam.tools](https://exam.tools) and sign in under **Assist with an Exam**.

2. Select **Profile** in the upper-left navigation.

3. Find **Saved Signature** and expand the section.

   ![ExamTools Profile page showing the Saved Signature section expanded with a signature preview and a New Signature button](/img/docs/account-setup/profile-saved-signature.png)

4. Select **New Signature**.

5. Draw a signature in the **Sign Here** box with a mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen.

   ![New Signature dialog showing the drawing area and the Clear Signature, Cancel, and Save controls](/img/docs/account-setup/profile-signature-canvas.png)

6. Select **Save**.

The saved image is a reusable representation for future documents, not a readiness or identity check. Updating it affects future signing only; it does not change signatures already applied to completed documents.

## Sign without saving a signature

When signing documents, select **Use New Signature** and leave **Save as default signature** unselected. See [Complete an Applicant](../exam-day/complete-applicant#signing-dialog) for the in-session signing workflow.

## Verify

Return to **Saved Signature** on your profile and confirm that the updated signature is available for future signing.
