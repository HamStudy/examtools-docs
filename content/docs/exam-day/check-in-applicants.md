Before an applicant can take an exam, they need to be added to the active session and have their identity verified.

## Prerequisites

- An [open session](open-session)

## Add an applicant who pre-registered

If the applicant pre-registered online, they appear in the **Pre-registered** list:

1. Find the applicant in the pre-registered list.
2. Verify their government-issued photo ID matches the name in ExamTools.
3. Click **Check In**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Pre-registered applicant list with Check In button>

## Add an applicant who did not pre-register

1. Click **Add Applicant** in the session dashboard.

   <SCREENSHOT: Add Applicant button>

2. Enter the applicant's callsign (if they are already licensed) or their FCC FRN (if they are a new applicant with no callsign yet).

3. ExamTools will look up their information from FCC records. Confirm the name matches their ID, then click **Add to Session**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Applicant lookup result>

4. For applicants with no FCC record yet (first-time test takers), enter their information manually.

## Select the element

After adding the applicant, select which exam element they are testing for today (Technician/Element 2, General/Element 3, or Extra/Element 4).

<SCREENSHOT: Element selection for applicant>

## Verify

The applicant appears in the session's applicant list with status **Checked In** and the correct element.
