Use this page when an exam is administered and graded outside ExamTools, including a printed exam graded by hand. A VE records the score in ExamTools so the applicant can use the managed digital-paperwork workflow.

If your team completes paper CSCEs and Form 605s and submits the session through your VEC's traditional process, do not record the result in ExamTools. Follow your established paper workflow instead.

## Prerequisites

- An [open ExamTools session](open-session) with one record for each applicant
- The exam materials required by the administering organization
- The answer key or other external grading process

For a printed exam generated by ExamTools, prepare the materials before exam day:

- [Generate exams with a Pre-determined Key](../paper-exams/generate-predetermined-key-exams) for an established VEC key or overlay format
- [Generate and Print Exams](../paper-exams/generate-and-print) for randomized exams with ExamTools-generated answer-key pages

The applicant does not need to connect to or check in to the session before testing or result entry.

## Administer and grade the exam

1. Administer the exam according to your VEC's procedures.
2. Keep exam booklets, completed answer sheets, and answer keys under VE control.
3. Grade the exam using the process required for the exam materials. For ExamTools-generated paper exams, follow [Grade Manually](../paper-exams/grade-manually).
4. Record the number of correct answers and retain any evidence required by your VEC.

Do not send the applicant to sign until they have completed all intended elements, including any computerized or GradeCam exams in the same session.

## Record the result

1. In the session view, expand the applicant's row and open the **Manage** menu.
2. Select **Record External Exam**.
3. In the dialog:
   - Select the **exam element**.
   - Enter the **score** as the number of correct answers.
   - Optionally enter an **Exam Identifier** and notes for your records.
4. Click **Record exam**.

ExamTools calculates the pass/fail result and attaches it to the applicant's record. It also reminds you to provide evidence of the exam when submitting to your VEC.

## Verify the result

The applicant's row shows **Pass** or **Fail** for the recorded element. Confirm the element and score before continuing.

## Connect the applicant for signing

After all of the applicant's intended exams are complete:

1. Open the applicant's PIN menu and select **Copy Applicant Login Link**.
2. Verify the recipient and send the link directly to that applicant through a private communication channel.
3. The applicant opens the link and submits a join request.
4. A VE verifies the applicant's identity and approves the request.
5. The applicant selects **Finish and Sign Forms**.

The applicant login link contains private access information and is specific to that applicant. Never post it in a group chat or public message. It is different from the public session registration link.
