This guide covers the exam process for computer-based (online) exams. For paper exams, see [Paper Exams](../paper-exams).

## Prerequisites

- An [open session](open-session) with the applicant [checked in](check-in-applicants)

## Start the exam

1. In the session dashboard, find the applicant and click **Start Exam**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Applicant row with Start Exam button>

2. ExamTools generates a randomized exam for the selected element. The applicant's exam interface opens.

3. The applicant takes the exam on their own device. Direct them to the exam URL shown on screen.

   <SCREENSHOT: Exam URL / QR code for applicant>

> **Remote sessions:** Confirm the applicant is sharing their screen and that VEs can see both the applicant and their exam screen before they begin.

## Monitor the exam

While the applicant is testing:

- The session dashboard shows the applicant's progress (questions answered, time elapsed).
- At least one VE must be monitoring the applicant at all times (in-person: visual observation; remote: video call).

<SCREENSHOT: Session dashboard showing exam in progress>

## Record the result

When the applicant submits their exam, ExamTools displays the score automatically.

1. Confirm the score with the applicant.
2. If they **passed**: click **Mark Passed**. The result is recorded and the form-signing process begins.
3. If they **did not pass**: click **Mark Failed**. The applicant may want to test for a lower element or try again at a future session.

   <SCREENSHOT: Pass/fail result screen>
