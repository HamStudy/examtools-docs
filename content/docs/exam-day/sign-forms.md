After each applicant's exam is complete (pass or fail), NCVEC Form 605 must be signed by the applicant and at least three VEs. ExamTools handles this digitally.

## Prerequisites

- An applicant with a recorded exam result in the active session
- All signing VEs must have a [signature profile set up](../account-setup/add-signature)

## Applicant signs Form 605

1. Present the applicant with the signing screen. They can sign on a shared device or on their own device using the link ExamTools provides.

   <SCREENSHOT: Form 605 applicant signing screen>

2. The applicant reviews their information and draws their signature, then clicks **Submit**.

## VEs sign Form 605

Each VE who is signing (minimum three) must sign the form:

1. In the session dashboard, click **Sign** next to the applicant's entry.

   <SCREENSHOT: VE signing button in session dashboard>

2. Review the form, confirm the information is correct, and click **Apply Signature**.

   <SCREENSHOT: VE signature confirmation screen>

3. Repeat for each signing VE.

> **Remote sessions:** VEs sign from their own devices. Each VE opens the session and clicks **Sign** on the applicant's form.

## Verify

After all required signatures are collected, the applicant's form status changes to **Signed**. The session dashboard shows the signature count.

## Passing applicants — certificate

For applicants who passed, ExamTools also generates a CSCE (Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination). This is signed by the same VEs who signed Form 605. The process is identical — the certificate signing prompt appears immediately after Form 605 is complete.
