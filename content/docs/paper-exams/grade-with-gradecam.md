GradeCam is a bubble-sheet scanning tool built into ExamTools. It reads completed answer sheets using your device's camera and automatically scores them against the answer key.

## Prerequisites

- Completed answer sheets from a [paper exam session](run-paper-exam-day)
- A device with a camera (phone, tablet, or laptop with webcam)
- The GradeCam answer sheets printed using the **GradeCam** option when generating the exam

## Steps

1. In your open session, click **Scan with GradeCam** (found in the paper exams or grading section of the session).

   <SCREENSHOT: Session view with Scan with GradeCam option>

2. Allow camera access when prompted.

3. Hold the completed answer sheet flat in front of the camera. Keep it well-lit and fully in frame.

   <SCREENSHOT: GradeCam camera view with answer sheet in frame>

4. GradeCam detects the sheet automatically and reads the answers. When it locks on, it shows a score.

   <SCREENSHOT: GradeCam result showing score>

5. Confirm the score and click **Accept**. The score is attached to the applicant whose exam number matches the sheet.

6. Repeat for each applicant's answer sheet.

## Troubleshooting

**GradeCam isn't reading the sheet:**
- Ensure the sheet is printed at actual size (not scaled)
- Improve lighting — avoid glare from direct overhead lights
- Hold the sheet flat and still; avoid shadows crossing the bubble grid

**The wrong applicant is matched:**
- Verify the exam number on the answer sheet matches the applicant in ExamTools
- Each generated exam has a unique number — do not mix up sheets between applicants
