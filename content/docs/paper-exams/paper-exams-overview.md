ExamTools can generate randomized paper exams for any license element. Each exam is unique — questions are drawn from the FCC question pool and arranged in a different order for every copy.

## When to use paper exams

Paper exams are appropriate when:

- Applicants do not have access to a computer or device during the session
- Your location has unreliable internet connectivity
- Your VEC or session policy requires paper exams
- You are running a backup paper exam alongside a computer exam session

## What you need

- A computer with internet access to generate the exam PDFs (this can be done before exam day)
- A printer capable of printing standard letter-size pages
- Enough printed copies for your applicants (plus a few extras)
- Answer sheets (printed from ExamTools along with the exam)
- A grading method: [GradeCam scanning](grade-with-gradecam) or [manual answer key comparison](grade-manually)

## How paper exams fit into ExamTools

Paper exams use the same session structure as computer exams. You create and open the session the same way — the difference is that instead of launching an online exam for each applicant, you hand them a printed booklet. After grading, you enter the scores manually into ExamTools.

All form signing (Form 605, CSCE) works exactly the same whether the exam was paper or computer-based.
