ExamTools can generate randomized paper exams for any license element. Each copy draws questions from the FCC question pool and places them in a different order. Choose the paper workflow that fits your grading, paperwork, equipment, and session needs.

## Choose a paper workflow

| Workflow | Use it when | Session and grading |
|----------|-------------|---------------------|
| [GradeCam](generate-and-print#gradecam-bubble-forms) | You want to scan printed bubble forms with a laptop webcam, smartphone camera, or another device camera. | Prepare the booklet and bubble form here, then follow [Give a GradeCam Exam](../exam-day/gradecam-exam) for managed-session administration. |
| [Pre-determined Key (generation only or managed session)](generate-predetermined-key-exams) | Your printed exams must match an established ARRL or VEC answer key or overlay grading template format. | Generate and print only, or use a managed session. This mode uses manual grading, not GradeCam. |
| [Manual Grading (generation only or managed session)](generate-and-print) | You want randomized printed exams with answer-key pages generated by ExamTools. | Grade answer sheets by hand. You can record results in an ExamTools-managed session or use your VEC's paper paperwork and submission process. |

## Consider your setup

- Generate exam PDFs before exam day from a computer with internet access, then print them on letter-size paper.
- For GradeCam, download and print the separate bubble forms and have a camera available for scanning.
- For manual grading, keep the printed answer key secure until it is needed.
- An ExamTools-managed session can include paper and computerized exams, including different elements for the same applicant.
- Generation-only use does not require an ExamTools session or applicant management. Use your VEC's answer sheets, forms, and submission procedures.

Device-based and GradeCam grading can collect anonymous question-performance data that can be shared with the Question Pool Committee (QPC) to help improve question pools.
