Exam day workflow for sessions using printed paper exams. The session setup steps (opening the session, checking in applicants) are identical to computer exams — the difference starts when it's time to administer the test.

## Prerequisites

- Printed exam booklets and answer sheets from [Generate and Print](generate-and-print)
- An [open session](../exam-day/open-session) with applicants [checked in](../exam-day/check-in-applicants)

## Administer the exam

1. Hand each applicant a printed exam booklet and the matching answer sheet. The exam number on the booklet and the answer sheet must match.

2. Instruct the applicant to:
   - Write their name on the answer sheet
   - Mark only one answer per question
   - Not mark in the exam booklet (so booklets can be reused)

3. Monitor the applicant for the duration of the exam. At least one VE must be able to observe the applicant at all times.

4. When the applicant finishes, collect the booklet and answer sheet together.

> Keep the exam booklet and answer sheet together until grading is complete — the exam number on both must match for accurate grading.
