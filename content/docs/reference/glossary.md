title: Glossary

---

**Applicant** — A person taking an amateur radio license exam.

**ARRL** — American Radio Relay League; one of the major VECs that participates in ExamTools.

**CSCE (Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination)** — The paper certificate given to an applicant who passes an exam element. Signed by the VEs and the applicant.

**CVE (Certified VE)** — Another term for a team lead or session manager in ExamTools. Used interchangeably with "team lead."

**Element** — The specific exam level being administered. Element 2 = Technician, Element 3 = General, Element 4 = Amateur Extra.

**ExamTools** — The web-based platform for administering amateur radio license exams, built by SignalStuff.

**FCC** — Federal Communications Commission; the US agency that issues amateur radio licenses.

**Form 605 (NCVEC Form 605)** — The official FCC application form completed by every applicant at an exam session. Signed by the applicant and at least three VEs.

**FRN (FCC Registration Number)** — A unique identifier assigned to every person or entity registered with the FCC. Used to look up applicants with no existing callsign.

**GradeCam** — A bubble-sheet scanning tool built into ExamTools used to automatically grade paper exam answer sheets.

**HamStudy** — The account and study platform that ExamTools uses for user login and identity.

**NCVEC** — National Conference of Volunteer Examiner Coordinators; the body that creates and maintains the FCC amateur radio question pools.

**Production** — The live ExamTools environment at exam.tools, used for real exam sessions.

**Sandbox** — The practice ExamTools environment at sandbox.exam.tools, used for training and testing.

**Session** — A scheduled exam event in ExamTools where one or more applicants test for a license.

**SignalStuff** — The company that develops and maintains ExamTools.

**Team** — A group of VEs organized under one or more team leads in ExamTools. All sessions are associated with a team.

**Team identifier** — A short unique code (3–10 characters) assigned to a team when it is created.

**Team lead** — A VE with owner or co-owner privileges in ExamTools. Also called a CVE. Can create sessions, manage the VE roster, and submit results.

**ULS (Universal Licensing System)** — The FCC's database of amateur radio licensees. ExamTools uses it to look up applicant information.

**VE (Volunteer Examiner)** — An accredited amateur radio operator who is qualified to administer amateur radio license exams.

**VEC (Volunteer Examiner Coordinator)** — A national organization that coordinates and accredits VEs and receives exam results. Common VECs include ARRL and W5YI.

**W5YI** — One of the major VECs that participates in ExamTools.
