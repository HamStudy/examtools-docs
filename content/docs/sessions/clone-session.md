title: Clone a Session
weight: 4
---

Cloning copies the settings of an existing session — team, elements, VE assignments, and configuration — into a new session with a new date. This is the fastest way to schedule recurring sessions.

## Prerequisites

- At least one existing session to clone from

## Steps

1. Go to **Sessions** and find the session you want to copy.
2. Click the **...** menu on that session and select **Clone Session**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Session list with clone option in the overflow menu>

3. Enter the new **date and time**.
4. Review the copied settings and adjust anything that has changed (location, VE list, etc.).
5. Click **Create**.

## Verify

The new session appears in your sessions list with status **Scheduled** and the new date.
