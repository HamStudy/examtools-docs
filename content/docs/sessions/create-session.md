Schedule a new exam session for your team.

## Prerequisites

- [Team lead privileges](../teams/become-team-lead)
- [A team with at least three VEs](../teams/manage-ve-roster)

## Steps

1. Sign in to [exam.tools](https://exam.tools) and click **Sessions** in the top navigation.

2. Click **New Session**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Sessions page with New Session button>

3. Select the **team** this session belongs to.

4. Enter the session **date and time**.

5. Select the **license elements** you will be offering (Technician, General, Extra — select all that apply).

6. Click **Create Session**.

   <SCREENSHOT: New session creation form>

## Verify

The session appears in your sessions list with status **Scheduled** and the correct date.
