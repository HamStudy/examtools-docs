Create a team in ExamTools to run sessions and manage your VE roster.

## Prerequisites

- [Team lead privileges](become-team-lead)

## Steps

1. Sign in to [exam.tools](https://exam.tools) and click **Teams** in the top navigation.

2. Click **Create Team**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Teams page with Create Team button highlighted>

3. Fill in the team details:

   - **Team Name** — A descriptive name for your team (e.g., your club name or VEC affiliate name).
   - **Team Identifier** — A short, unique code (3–10 characters, letters and numbers only). Applicants use this to find your team. **This cannot be changed after creation.**
   - **VEC** — Select the VEC your team is affiliated with.

   <SCREENSHOT: Create team form>

4. Click **Create**.

## Verify

After creation, your team appears on the Teams page. Click into it to confirm the name, identifier, and VEC are correct.
