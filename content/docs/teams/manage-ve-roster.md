Your team's VE roster controls who can participate in your sessions and what they can do. You need at least three VEs (including yourself) to administer an exam session.

## Prerequisites

- [A team you own or co-own](create-team)

## Add a VE to your team

1. Go to [exam.tools/teams](https://exam.tools/teams) and open your team.

2. Click **Add VE**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Team page with Add VE button>

3. Search for the VE by callsign. They must already have an [ExamTools account](../account-setup/create-account).

4. Select their name from the results and choose their initial role:

   | Role | Use when |
   |------|----------|
   | **VE** | Standard team member — can join sessions and sign forms |
   | **VE Admin** | Trusted VE who also needs to manage applicant info |
   | **Co-owner** | Another team lead with full management access |

5. Click **Add to Team**.

   <SCREENSHOT: Add VE confirmation dialog>

## Change a VE's role

1. Find the VE in the team member list.
2. Click the role badge next to their name.
3. Select the new role from the dropdown.

## Remove a VE from your team

1. Find the VE in the team member list.
2. Click the **...** menu next to their name and select **Remove from Team**.

> Removing a VE from the team does not affect sessions they already participated in.

## Verify

The team member list shows each VE's callsign, name, and current role. Confirm the roster is correct before your session date.
