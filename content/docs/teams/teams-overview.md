A **team** in ExamTools is a group of Volunteer Examiners that run exam sessions together. Every session in ExamTools belongs to a team.

## Team roles

Teams have a role hierarchy that controls what each member can do:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full control — can edit team settings, manage all members, create and delete sessions |
| **Co-owner** | Same as owner, except cannot remove the owner |
| **VE Admin** | Can manage the VE roster and edit applicant information in sessions |
| **VE** | Can join sessions, proctor exams, and sign forms |

The terms "team lead" and "CVE" both refer to users with owner or co-owner roles.

## How teams relate to VECs

A team is affiliated with one VEC. The VEC determines which sessions you can submit results to. If your team's VEs are accredited through multiple VECs, each VEC relationship is managed separately within ExamTools.

## Team identifier

Each team has a short, unique identifier (3–10 characters) that applicants and VEs use to find or join the team. You choose this when you create the team and it cannot be changed later.
